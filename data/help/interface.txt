<c=white>Staying alive<c=/>

You get hungry and thirsty quickly (examine a pond or river to drink from it or fill your bottles), and you need to sleep every night (<c=magenta>$<c=/>).  You heal slowly on your own, faster while asleep, and not at all when starving or parched.  Go without water for two days, or food for six, and your body starts to fail.  Sleeping, reading and other long tasks stop when danger comes near or you are hurt.

The game saves itself every two hours of game time, and when you quit.  Death is permanent: it ends the game and deletes the save.

<c=white>Getting off the island<c=/>

Listen to the radio from your wreck.  The wreck itself is on the beach where you woke: examine it (<c=magenta>x<c=/>) to see what it needs to sail again.  Materials count if you carry them or they lie within two steps of you.

Press <c=magenta>/<c=/> on the map to search for a place by name.  The small map beside the view flags your errand's target with a <c=magenta>!<c=/>.

<c=white>People and factions<c=/>

Walk into people to talk to them.  Camp folk give errands and gossip; the four factions (the <c=magenta>Cultists<c=/>, the <c=green>Military<c=/>, the <c=brown>Naturalists<c=/> and the <c=ltblue>Scientists<c=/>) give orders, and let you join.  Errands send you across the island: look over a place, clear out a raider camp, fetch a supply case, carry a sealed letter, or bring a corner substation back to life.  Report back and you're paid on account.  Members buy from their faction's counter, rise in rank, and are hated by the factions theirs is at odds with.

In story mode the factions race each other for the Lab.  The radio and anyone you talk to will tell you how close they are.  If a rival takes it, you have seven days to cut its power at the plant, or the island is theirs.

<c=white>The Lab<c=/>

The Lab's blast door needs power, the Control Station's remote release, and a passcard.  Get the power plant running (you'll need a wrench), close the breakers at the Lab's substation, throw the switch at the Control Station, and find one of the four passcards: one in an old bunker, one aboard a wreck on a far shore, two with Lab staff who fled into the towns.  Ask around: people know where.  At the core you can close the seam for good, or, if your faction trusts you, hand the Lab to them and hold it for three days.
