Welcome to <c=ltgreen>Cataclysm II<c=/>!  Whales wrote it from 2013 to 2016 as the sequel to Cataclysm, with a plan for a game you can win.  The Asciiverse continues it from Whales' notes and development blog.

You wake on the beach of an island, beside the wreck of your boat.  Survive, and find your way off.
